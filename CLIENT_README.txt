========================================================================
                      IQ MOTORS POS - QUICK START GUIDE
========================================================================

Thank you for using IQ Motors POS! This desktop application allows you to
create professional invoices, automatically calculate 20% VAT on parts,
email invoices directly to customers with attached PDFs, and look up
Mercedes-Benz genuine parts & List Prices (LP) in the background.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO INSTALL (FIRST TIME ONLY)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

FOR WINDOWS:
1. Double-click "IQ-Motors-POS-Setup-1.0.0.exe".
2. If Windows shows a blue alert ("Windows protected your PC"):
   - Click "More info"
   - Click "Run anyway"
3. The app will install automatically and put an "IQ Motors POS" icon on
   your desktop.

FOR MAC:
1. Double-click "IQ-Motors-POS-Setup-1.0.0.dmg".
2. Drag the "IQ Motors POS" icon into your Applications folder.
3. Open Applications, right-click "IQ Motors POS", and click "Open".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ONE-TIME SETUP (SETTINGS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Open the app and click the "Settings" tab at the top:

A. Business Details:
   - Your VAT number (228706791) and invoice numbering are already set.
   - All generated invoice PDFs are saved automatically to:
     Documents / IQ Motors Invoices

B. Mercedes-Benz B2B Portal (Connect Once):
   - Under "Mercedes-Benz B2B Connect", click "Open Mercedes B2B Login".
   - Sign in with your workshop account once.
   - Once signed in, you can close that window.
   - Your session will stay active permanently so all future part
     searches run silently in the background!

C. Automated Email Sending (Optional):
   - If you want the app to email invoices directly to customers from
     your Gmail, enter your Gmail address and 16-character App Password.
   - Click "Test email login" to verify it works.
   - Click "Save settings".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CREATING AN INVOICE & LOOKING UP MERCEDES PARTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Go to the "New invoice" tab.
2. Enter Customer Name, Phone, Email, and Vehicle Reg (e.g. YA67VCT).
3. To look up a Mercedes Part:
   - Type the part number into the "Part number" box (e.g. A0008280388).
   - Click "Search Mercedes LP price".
   - The app searches the Mercedes catalog in the background and
     automatically enters the real Part Name and exact List Price (LP).
4. Add any Labour or manual parts as needed.
   - Notice: 20% VAT is automatically applied only to parts.
5. Click "Save PDF" to create the invoice, or click
   "Save PDF & email to customer" to send it immediately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. VIEWING PAST INVOICES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Click the "Invoices" tab at the top to see a list of all saved invoices.
Click "Open PDF" next to any invoice to view or print it anytime.
